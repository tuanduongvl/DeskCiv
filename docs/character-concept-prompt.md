Create character concept art for DeskCiv, a calm city-builder simulation shown as a desktop wallpaper.

Use your built-in image generation tool for every image. Do not use Python, SVG, Blender, canvas, procedural geometry, or any script to draw the art. If image generation is unavailable, stop and report that. Do not substitute another method.

Work only in the current directory. Do not read, edit, or create files elsewhere, except to copy your generated images from the image tool's output location into this directory.

## Character

An original adult woodcutter from a fictional Neolithic river village.

- Weathered man in his thirties, believable human proportions, about 7 heads tall, sturdy working build.
- Short dark beard, tied-back hair, kind but tired expression.
- Layered clothing made by hand: undyed wool tunic, moss-green hooded short cape, hide vest, leather belt with bone toggle, woven leg wraps, soft hide boots.
- Tool: a large stone axe with a polished flint head lashed to an ash-wood handle with rawhide.
- Small details: wood chips on the clothing, a coil of rope and a small pouch at the belt.
- No metal, buttons, zippers, fantasy armor, modern clothing, text, logos, or watermark.

## Quality target

Polished production art for a premium PC city-builder: appealing, clean silhouette, believable materials, soft painterly lighting, rich but natural earth tones, and enough detail to feel finished. His job must be obvious from silhouette and props alone, even when small on screen.

This must be an original design. Do not imitate or reference any existing game, studio, franchise, character, or artist.

## Images to create

1. `01-orthographic-turnaround.png`, 1536x1024 or the closest available landscape size. One model sheet of the same character in a neutral relaxed A-pose, with the axe shown separately beside him. Show true orthographic front, left side, and back views at identical scale, aligned at head, shoulders, hips, knees, and feet. No perspective. Plain light-gray background, even lighting, full body visible, no labels.
2. `02-isometric-idle.png`, 1024x1024. The same character standing with the axe resting on his shoulder, full body, true 2:1 isometric game camera from above at about 30 degrees, facing the lower-right. Plain light-gray background with a soft contact shadow.
3. `03-isometric-chopping.png`, 1024x1024. The same character from the same isometric camera at the top of a powerful axe swing beside a partly cut tree trunk, with wood chips in the air. Full body visible, clear action silhouette.

Use image 1 as the visual reference for images 2 and 3 so that face, clothing, colors, proportions, and axe stay consistent. Regenerate an image if the character changes, any limb or hand is malformed, the full body is cropped, or text appears.

## Finish

Inspect all three final images yourself. Write `REPORT.md` with the image tool used, the final prompt for each image, each file's pixel size, and any remaining inconsistencies. Be honest about flaws. Stop after this. Do not create 3D models.
